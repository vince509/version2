ABOUT THE PROJECT

Research Hub

Research Hub is a private local repository for STI College Alaminos students to discover, organize, and review research work. The platform keeps the library focused on students and faculty by making it easy to upload papers, search by keyword or metadata, and review new submissions before publication.

Purpose

Preserve academic work in a single searchable place and make it easier to discover relevant studies.

Features

Search by titles, topics, courses, authors, and abstract content with a shared local repository view.

Access

Accounts are managed by the system administrator, with private access and local storage for the project.

Team

Built for STI College Alaminos to support research collaboration and digital access to academic collections.
